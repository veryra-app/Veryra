Bad class day toolkit

  1. Name it: "Today was a hard class" is a fact, not a verdict on you as a dancer.
  2. Write down ONE thing that went a little better than last time — even a tiny thing counts.
  3. Ask: what is one small thing I can practise for 5 minutes tomorrow?
  4. Rest is training too. Eat, hydrate, and let your body recover.
  5. Remember why you started. One class is one page, not the whole story. 💛

Privacy, in plain words

Privacy, in plain words

When you create an account, Veryra saves your goals, progress notes, coach questions, and mindset check-ins under your account only. Other users cannot see your rows — the database is set so each signed-in person can only read and change their own data.

Guest mode keeps nothing: if you use Veryra without an account, your goals and notes live only in the open tab and disappear when you close it.

Do not put private personal details (your full name, address, school, or passwords) in goals or notes. You can delete your goals and check-ins in the app at any time.
